\section*{Remarks}

\textbf{Line spacing (Notice: specification, claims and abstract).} The specification, claims and abstract are now double spaced on every page.

\textbf{Abstract on a separate sheet.} The abstract has been moved from the front of the specification to its own sheet following the claims, and shortened to fewer than 150 words.

\textbf{Excessive text in FIGS.\ 6, 7, 16 and 17.} The explanatory paragraphs previously printed on the sheets bearing FIGS.\ 6 and 7 have been moved, verbatim, into the detailed description of the specification. Former FIGS.\ 16, 17 and 18 were computer program listings; former FIGS.\ 16 and 17 each exceed 300 lines and, under 37 CFR 1.96(c), are now submitted as a computer program listing appendix in ASCII text, together with former FIG.\ 18. An incorporation-by-reference paragraph identifying each appendix file by name, date of creation and size in bytes has been added to the specification under 37 CFR 1.77(b)(5), and the descriptions formerly given in the captions and the brief description of those figures now appear in that paragraph. Characters outside the ASCII set in the original listings are transliterated as stated at the top of each file.

\textbf{Font too small in FIGS.\ 14 and 15.} The flowcharts of former FIGS.\ 14 and 15 have been redrawn with every node, label and decision outcome unchanged, using lettering with capital height of at least 0.32 cm, and continued across lettered sheets joined by numbered off-page connectors. All other drawing sheets have likewise been reset so that all lettering is at least 0.32 cm in height.

\textbf{Unnumbered or misnumbered figures (FIGS.\ 14, 16, 17 and 18).} Every drawing sheet now carries a figure label, and every figure continued onto additional sheets is labeled with a capital letter suffix. The brief description of the drawings has been amended to correspond. The figure numbering maps as follows:
